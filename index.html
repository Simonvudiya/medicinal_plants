Medicinal Plants Directory

Medicinal Plants of East Africa

Discover, learn, and document the medicinal plants of East Africa.

This app is a digital resource for exploring medicinal plants found across East Africa, including their local names, botanical identification, traditional uses, distribution, plant parts used, and relevant scientific information.

Designed for students, researchers, farmers, herbal practitioners, conservationists, and the general public, the platform brings traditional knowledge and modern botanical information together in one accessible place.

Explore plants. Preserve knowledge. Support research.

About the Author

Simon Vudiya

Simon Vudiya is an agricultural researcher and plant health professional with a background in horticulture and plant pathology. His interests span plant health, agricultural research, data analysis, GIS, and technology for agriculture. Through this medicinal plants project, he aims to contribute to the documentation, preservation, and accessible sharing of knowledge about East Africa's diverse medicinal plant resources.
